Privacy Policy

Ticket Trader
Support Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 7, 2026

Ticket Trader ("we", "us") is an app that helps fans trade event tickets with each other. This policy explains what information the app collects, how it's used, and the choices you have.

Information we collect

Information you give us

  • Account details: your email address, password (stored securely by our login provider; we can't see it), display name, and when you agreed to this policy.
  • Profile details: your home city, if you add it.
  • Your teams and sports: the teams and sports you choose to follow, and any teams you say you hold season tickets for. Only you can see them.
  • Transfer details: the name and email you want tickets sent to. Only people whose trade you've accepted can see these.
  • Payment details: the usernames, phone numbers or emails you add for Venmo, Cash App, PayPal, Apple Cash or Zelle; the cash amount in a trade; and when each person marked it sent or received. We never see, handle or hold the payment itself.
  • Tickets: the events, sections, rows, seats, and ticket sites (for example Ticketmaster) of tickets you add.
  • Ticket prices: what you paid per ticket and the seating area (lower, club or upper), if you add them. What you paid is private: it's only used to estimate how even a trade is, nobody else ever sees it, and it's deleted when the ticket changes hands.
  • Trades: offers you send and receive, their messages, when an offer was first seen, each step of the ticket transfer, and deadlines.
  • Trade problems: what you write when you report a problem with a trade or add your side. The other person in the trade can read this too. If you email us screenshots, we keep them in our support inbox, not in the app.
  • Account standing: how many of your trades failed, and whether your account is under review or suspended.
  • Messages: chats you send to other members, and when each person last read a chat.
  • Ratings and reviews: stars and comments you leave after a trade, and those others leave about you.
  • Reports and blocks: people you report or block, and why.
  • Trade settings: whether you've turned on last-minute trades, and when.
  • Notification settings: whether you want push notifications, and a push token for each phone you sign in on, so alerts reach the right device.

Information collected automatically

  • Basic technical information needed to run the service, such as when you sign in. We don't use advertising trackers and we don't track your movements. If you choose to use your location for Near you, your phone uses your rough location once. It stays on your phone, and we don't receive or store it. You can pick a city instead, and you can remove your area any time in Profile.

We never ask for or store your Ticketmaster or other ticket-site passwords. You transfer tickets yourself in those apps.

How we use information

  • To run the app: show your tickets, send trade offers, guide transfers, deliver messages, and show ratings.
  • To keep the community safe: review reports, decide trade disputes, enforce blocks and deadlines, limit spam, and prevent fraud.
  • To send push notifications about your offers, trades, messages, and ratings, if you allow them. You can turn these off any time in Profile → Notifications or in your phone's settings.
  • To contact you about your account.

We don't sell your personal information, and we don't share it for advertising.

What other members can see

  • Your display name, home city, the date you joined, your number of completed trades, your number of failed trades, your average rating, and reviews about you.
  • Your listed tickets.
  • Your messages, only to the person you're chatting with. They can also see when you've read their messages and when you first opened their trade offer.
  • Your transfer name and email, only to the other person in a trade you've accepted.
  • Your payment usernames, only to someone paying you cash in a trade you've accepted.

Payment apps

When you tap a payment button, the app opens Venmo, Cash App or PayPal with the amount and the other person's username filled in. You pay in that app, under its own terms and privacy policy. We don't receive your payment account details or card numbers.

What Ticket Trader staff can see

To review problems, our admins can see trades under review (both people's names, the tickets, each transfer step, and what each person wrote), reports, and accounts that are under review or suspended. Their decisions are logged. Admins don't see your chat messages or your transfer name and email through these tools.

Service providers

We use a small number of companies to run the app, and they only process data on our behalf:

  • Supabase: database, sign-in, and live updates.
  • Expo: app builds and delivering push notifications.
  • Apple and Google: app distribution (App Store, TestFlight, Google Play) and delivering push notifications to your phone (Apple Push Notification service and Firebase Cloud Messaging). A notification's text, such as the start of a chat message, passes through these services to reach you. Google also handles our account emails, such as sign-up confirmations and password reset codes, through Gmail.

How long we keep information

We keep your information while your account is open. When you delete your account, we delete your profile, tickets, trades, ratings, messages, transfer details, and push tokens. Reports you filed may be kept without your name so we can finish reviewing them. You can't delete your account while one of your trades is under review. A record of admin decisions may be kept after your account is deleted, without your name.

Your choices

  • Edit: you can change your transfer details on the Profile tab.
  • Block or report: you can block or report anyone from a chat's ⋯ menu, or report them from their profile.
  • Notifications: turn push notifications on or off in Profile → Notifications.
  • Delete: go to Profile → Delete account to permanently delete your account and data right away.
  • Questions or requests: email us at our support address.

Children

Ticket Trader isn't meant for children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect information from them.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the effective date above and, for significant changes, let you know in the app.
