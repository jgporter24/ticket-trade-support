Privacy Policy

Ticket Trader
Support Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 2, 2026

Ticket Trader ("we", "us") is an app that helps fans trade event tickets with each other. This policy explains what information the app collects, how it's used, and the choices you have.

Information we collect

Information you give us

  • Account details: your email address, password (stored securely by our login provider; we can't see it), and display name.
  • Profile details: your home city, if you add it.
  • Transfer details: the name and email you want tickets sent to. Only people whose trade you've accepted can see these.
  • Tickets: the events, sections, rows, seats, and ticket sites (for example Ticketmaster) of tickets you add.
  • Trades: offers you send and receive, their messages, and each step of the ticket transfer.
  • Messages: chats you send to other members.
  • Ratings and reviews: stars and comments you leave after a trade, and those others leave about you.
  • Reports and blocks: people you report or block, and why.
  • Notification settings: whether you want push notifications, and a push token for each phone you sign in on, so alerts reach the right device.

Information collected automatically

  • Basic technical information needed to run the service, such as when you sign in. We don't use advertising trackers and we don't track your location.

We never ask for or store your Ticketmaster or other ticket-site passwords. You transfer tickets yourself in those apps.

How we use information

  • To run the app: show your tickets, send trade offers, guide transfers, deliver messages, and show ratings.
  • To keep the community safe: review reports, enforce blocks, and prevent fraud.
  • To send push notifications about your offers, trades, messages, and ratings, if you allow them. You can turn these off any time in Profile → Notifications or in your phone's settings.
  • To contact you about your account.

We don't sell your personal information, and we don't share it for advertising.

What other members can see

  • Your display name, home city, the date you joined, your number of completed trades, your average rating, and reviews about you.
  • Your listed tickets.
  • Your messages, only to the person you're chatting with.
  • Your transfer name and email, only to the other person in a trade you've accepted.

Service providers

We use a small number of companies to run the app, and they only process data on our behalf:

  • Supabase: database, sign-in, and live updates.
  • Expo: app builds and delivering push notifications.
  • Apple and Google: app distribution (App Store, TestFlight, Google Play) and delivering push notifications to your phone (Apple Push Notification service and Firebase Cloud Messaging). A notification's text, such as the start of a chat message, passes through these services to reach you.

How long we keep information

We keep your information while your account is open. When you delete your account, we delete your profile, tickets, trades, ratings, messages, transfer details, and push tokens. Reports you filed may be kept without your name so we can finish reviewing them.

Your choices

  • Edit: you can change your transfer details on the Profile tab.
  • Block or report: you can block or report anyone from a chat's ⋯ menu, or report them from their profile.
  • Notifications: turn push notifications on or off in Profile → Notifications.
  • Delete: go to Profile → Delete account to permanently delete your account and data right away.
  • Questions or requests: email us at our support address.

Children

Ticket Trader isn't meant for children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect information from them.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the effective date above and, for significant changes, let you know in the app.
