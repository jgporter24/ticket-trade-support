How trading works

  1. Add the tickets you own, and pick the site they're on (for example Ticketmaster, AXS, or SeatGeek).
  2. Find an event, tap Trade on someone's tickets, and offer some of yours.
  3. When an offer is accepted, each of you sends your tickets by the deadline, using that ticket site's own transfer feature. Ticket Trader shows you the steps and the name and email to send to.
  4. Each person confirms in the app when their tickets arrive. Then you can rate each other.

What happens if…

How long do I have to send my tickets?

When an offer is accepted, you each have 48 hours to send, or until 24 hours before the event if that's sooner. If an offer is accepted very close to that cutoff, you still get at least 6 hours. The trade screen shows your deadline, and you'll get a reminder 12 hours before it. Offers can't be sent or accepted within 24 hours of the event.

The other person never sent their tickets and I haven't sent mine.

When the deadline passes, the trade fails and your tickets go back to being available. The person who didn't send gets a failed trade on their profile, and you can rate them. If neither of you sent, it counts for both of you. If you can't send because the other person never added where to receive tickets, it counts against them, not you.

I sent my tickets, but the other person didn't send theirs.

The trade moves to Under review at the deadline, and we work out where the tickets ended up. Don't confirm tickets you haven't received. If it can't wait, tap Report a problem with this trade right away.

Tickets were sent, but nobody confirmed them.

You get one extra day after the deadline to confirm. After that, the trade moves to Under review.

I need to back out of a trade.

Tap Withdraw from trade. You can withdraw until tickets have been sent to you or a transfer has been confirmed, and it doesn't count as a failed trade. If you already sent your tickets, cancel that transfer on the ticket site first, because Ticket Trader can't pull tickets back. Once tickets have been sent to you, confirm them or report a problem instead.

How do I report a problem with a trade?

On the trade screen, tap Report a problem with this trade and describe what happened. The trade pauses, deadlines stop, and tickets stay locked while we review it. Each person can add their side, and the other person can read it. To share screenshots, email them to our support address and quote the trade reference shown in the app. We don't store screenshots in the app.

Why does my trade have rounds?

When a trade includes more than one event, each of you sends one event at a time, soonest first. The next round opens once both of you confirm the current one, so the most anyone can lose is one exchange. If a later round fails, finished rounds stay finished.

What happens after failed trades?

Failed trades show on your profile. At 2 failures, we review your account. At 3, it's suspended: you can't send or accept offers or start new chats, but you can finish trades already in progress. If you think a failure was a mistake, email us.

Frequently asked questions

Does Ticket Trader hold my tickets or my money?

No. Trades are ticket-for-ticket swaps between members. Tickets move directly between your ticket-site accounts, and Ticket Trader never takes payments.

Do you need my Ticketmaster (or other ticket site) password?

No, never. You transfer tickets yourself in that site's app. Ticket Trader will never ask for your password.

Who can see my transfer name and email?

Only the other person in a trade you've accepted. You can set or change them on the Profile tab under "Where should people send you tickets?"

Who can I message?

You can start a chat with someone once one of you has sent the other a trade offer.

How do I report or block someone?

In a chat, tap the ⋯ menu and choose Report or Block. You can also tap Report at the bottom of anyone's profile. We review every report.

How do I turn notifications on or off?

Use the Notifications switch on the Profile tab, or your phone's Settings app.

How do I delete my account?

In the app, go to Profile and tap Delete account at the bottom. This permanently deletes your account, tickets, trades, ratings, and messages right away. If a trade is in progress and tickets have already been sent, or a trade is under review, finish that trade first. If you can't sign in, email us and we'll delete it for you.

I forgot my password.

Email us from the address on your account and we'll help you get back in.
