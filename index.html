How trading works

  1. Add the tickets you own, and pick the site they're on (for example Ticketmaster, AXS, or SeatGeek).
  2. Find an event, tap Trade on someone's tickets, and offer some of yours.
  3. When an offer is accepted, each of you sends your tickets using that ticket site's own transfer feature. Ticket Trader shows you the steps and the name and email to send to.
  4. Each person confirms in the app when their tickets arrive. Then you can rate each other.

Frequently asked questions

Does Ticket Trader hold my tickets or my money?

No. Trades are ticket-for-ticket swaps between members. Tickets move directly between your ticket-site accounts, and Ticket Trader never takes payments.

Do you need my Ticketmaster (or other ticket site) password?

No, never. You transfer tickets yourself in that site's app. Ticket Trader will never ask for your password.

Who can see my transfer name and email?

Only the other person in a trade you've accepted. You can set or change them on the Profile tab under "Where should people send you tickets?"

The other person never sent their tickets. What should I do?

Don't mark the trade as received. Message them from the trade screen, and if it isn't resolved, report them (see below) and email us with the trade details.

How do I report or block someone?

In a chat, tap the ⋯ menu and choose Report or Block. You can also tap Report at the bottom of anyone's profile. We review every report.

How do I turn notifications on or off?

Use the Notifications switch on the Profile tab, or your phone's Settings app.

How do I delete my account?

In the app, go to Profile and tap Delete account at the bottom. This permanently deletes your account, tickets, trades, ratings, and messages right away. If a trade is in progress and tickets have already been sent, finish that trade first. If you can't sign in, email us and we'll delete it for you.

I forgot my password.

Email us from the address on your account and we'll help you get back in.
